## Philosophy

These tutorials follow David Perkins' "Making Learning Whole" methodology:

> "If teaching baseball, you first take people to a game or let them play, rather than teaching them to wind twine to make a baseball or the physics of parabolas."

Each tutorial:
1. **Starts with working code** - Run it, see results
2. **Builds intuition** - What just happened?
3. **Goes deeper** - The mathematics and mechanics
4. **Provides foundations** - History and further reading
